# How interview times are proposed
The assistant looks for times when the candidate and every interviewer are free, inside each person's own working hours and time zone, Monday to Friday. It proposes up to three times that do not overlap.

## Who approves the invite
A recruiter reads every invite draft, picks one time and approves it. Nothing is sent and nothing is booked before that.

## When no time works
If there is no time when everyone is free, the assistant says so and a recruiter works it out with the people involved.
