# Interview day guide (candidates)
Larkspur Robotics is a made-up company used for this demo.

## What to bring to the onsite interview
Bring a photo ID for the front desk and a laptop if you want to show past work. No printed resume is needed.

## Parking and where to go
Park in the visitor lot on the north side of the building. Check in at the front desk in the main lobby; the recruiter meets you there.

## Video interviews
Video interviews use the link in your invite. Join two minutes early to test sound and camera.
