# Opening: cloud engineer (platform team)
The platform team has one open seat for a cloud engineer who builds AWS infrastructure with Terraform and runs Kubernetes clusters for internal services.

## What the work looks like
You review infrastructure changes, keep deploys repeatable, and share the on-call rotation for the platform. You write down what broke and what changed after every incident.

## Signals we look for
Reviewed infrastructure as code, calm on-call habits, and clear written notes.
