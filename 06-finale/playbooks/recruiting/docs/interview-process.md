# Interview process (candidate guide)
After you apply, a recruiter reads your resume and replies within five business days.

## Next step after you apply
The next step is a work-sample interview: four short tasks about connecting to the work, judging an output, working within limits, and owning a result. You see a consent notice first and can stop at any time. A person reads every answer and makes every decision.
