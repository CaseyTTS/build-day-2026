# Hardware runbook
## Laptop overheating or shutting down
A loud fan with shutdowns during calls usually means blocked vents or a failing fan. Keep the laptop on a hard surface and check the battery health report. If shutdowns continue, desk-side support swaps the laptop and moves your files.

## Printers
Printer jams go to desk-side support with the printer's asset tag.
