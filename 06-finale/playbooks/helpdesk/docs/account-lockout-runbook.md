# Account lockout and password reset runbook
After five failed sign-ins the account locks for fifteen minutes.

## Self-service reset
Use the password reset page from any browser and verify with your registered MFA method. A reset never needs the help desk to know your password.

## After an MFA reset
If you are locked out after an MFA reset, the identity team re-enrolls your device after confirming who you are by a video call. The help desk never turns MFA off to unlock an account.
